To avoid confusion with " five . " Try reading long strings of numbers over a dodgy radio and having someone else copy them down . Saying " niner " makes it way easier to comprehend on the other end . To add - this is the same reason you 'd say , " alpha , bravo , charlie , delta , echo , foxtrot " rather than " ay , bee , cee , dee , ee , eff " - B , C , D , E all sound very similar over the radio .
