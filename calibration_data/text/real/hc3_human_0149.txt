The constitution says : > No person shall be held to answer for a capital , or otherwise infamous crime , unless on a presentment or indictment of a Grand Jury , except in cases arising in the land or naval forces , or in the Militia , when in actual service in time of War or public danger In otherwords : Someone accused of committing murder has to have a Grand Jury say there 's probable cause for a trial . So : Step 1 : Grand Jury : is there enough evidence to have a trial for murder . Step 2 : Regular Jury : is he guilty of murder ? You do n't hear about Grand Juries because in over 99 % of cases brought , Grand Juries indict . Partially , because you only need 12 of 16 - 23 members to support indictment . At the federal level , [ 538 reports ] ( URL_0 ) 161,989 of 162,000 federal grand juries indict .
