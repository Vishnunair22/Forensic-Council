I can think of three reasons : Firstly , lakes are smaller , and therefore there is a greater chance that the capsule might miss the lake . If the capsule aimed for the Great Lakes , and missed , it might fall on Detroit , Chicago , Toronto , and other large cities . Also , the smaller size of the lakes means the many ships travelling within them are more compact . The odds of hitting a random cargo ship in the ocean is small , but the chance of hitting a cargo ship in the lakes is a bit higher . Secondly , there are not nearly as many recovery ships in lakes as there are in the ocean . If the capsule got lost , they could send a whole fleet of warships from many nations to find it . On the lakes , there are only a few coast guard vessels available . Thirdly , inland waterways , such as lakes , have strict anti - pollution regulations , more so than in the open ocean . If the capsule sank in the lakes , then NASA could face repercussion form the EPA .
