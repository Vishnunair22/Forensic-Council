* USA : Menchie 's ; South Africa : Wakaberry * UK : Marks & Spencer ; South Africa : Woolworths A lot of thought goes into naming brands . There are marketing agencies who actually sell names to businesses ! I believe , then , that products get different names in different territories for a vast number of reasons . In the Menchie 's example above , I would say the name Menchie 's would n't make the least bit of sense to South Africans , because Menchie is n't a name that exists here . " Wakaberry " sounds funky and healthy , which ticks a lot of boxes for people here .
