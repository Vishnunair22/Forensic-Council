Plastics . If you take a look at a lot of older appliances , they were largely metal . And they were expensive . But it was thought that if you bought it , you pretty much bought it for life . You 'd repair as needed , as the original large investment in the product meant it would be worth the cost of fixing it rather than buying a new one . Once plastics became widely used , products became cheaper . So cheap , in fact , that it made more sense to buy a new product rather than fix the old one , which would cost as much or more to repair than replace .
