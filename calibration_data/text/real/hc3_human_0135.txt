Answering from personal experience . They have a " kit " containing hundreds of different common allergens and use a special tool to " inject " the allergens into your skin , in my case they " injected " my back . Then they wait 10 - 15 minutes for a reaction to occur . The areas that turn red indicate you are allergic to whatever was injected there . For allergies like peanuts many people find out by accident usually when they are young and the allergy has not yet fully developed . They swell up and feel funny after eating whatever it is then go to the doctor who tells them they had an allergic reaction .
