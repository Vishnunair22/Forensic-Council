They buy up debt for cents on the dollar . Take for instance some debt that has been recently charged off by a bank . It is n't fiscally profitable for the bank to try and collect on it because they do n't have or want to maintain a huge collections center . But they could sell it for ten cents on the dollar for someone else to collect . In the case of $ 1,000,000 of debt , the bank gets back $ 100,000 and the debt buyer gets $ 1,000,000 to try and collect . As long as they can keep their margins above 10 % it is all profit , minus whatever overhead they are incurring .
