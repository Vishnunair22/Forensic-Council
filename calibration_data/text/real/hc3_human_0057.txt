* * Disclaimer : I am a PC builder who has used all AMD components since 2009 . * * Previous commenters are totally correct as far as the business - side of things go , but AMD as a company has a couple of unique characteristics that keep it in business . The first is that in the early 2000s they invented the x86/64 architecture , and because of it 's superiority to Intel 's x86 architecture , Intel has paid them to use it for their own processors , and has ever since , meaning that for every processor Intel sells , AMD gets some of that money for the Architecture licensing . Additionally , AMD has contracts with Microsoft , Sony , and Nintendo for their next - gen gaming systems , giving them long - term gurantees .
