The world is moving to an electronic cash world . Employees and employers both prefer direct deposit , it 's cheaper and easier to manage . Distributing pay checks takes time . I work for a small business that has 50 employees , at 3 locations . On pay day , someone has to go to each location and distribute checks to everyone , that takes them almost the entire day . Imagine a factory with 1000 employees , at the end of every shift 333 employees will be lining up for pay checks . If the payroll people spend 30 seconds per employee , that 's still 2.5 hours spent on distributing pay checks every shift . If you distribute cash , it 's even worse . Ask older vets about payroll day at a military base . Multiple armored cars loaded with a few million in cash need a military police escorts . Heavily armed guards managing lines of soldiers trying to get their pay . Armored cars carrying pay roll used to get robbed all the time , checks and direct deposit eliminate all the costs and security issues surrounding having large amounts of cash on site .
