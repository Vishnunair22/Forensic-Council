AFAIK , high level endurance , strength , and other physical activities require a tremendous amount of energy . As another individual has mentioned , many metabolic processes require energy to regulate . However , when your body enters one of these hyper - activity states , all hands are on deck to get the best performance . Digestion stops , heart rate increases , breathing increases . The upset stomach / puking , is like due to a combination of stomach contents not being fully digested ( tossed around in the stomach ) , and or the pausing / resuming of basic digestive processes .
