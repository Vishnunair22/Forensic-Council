The places where the fire started are usually places where the fire was " coolest " . As the fire grows it becomes hotter and hotter . The way things burn and melt changes based on heat . By analyzing the way the fire affected various materials the investigators can often pinpoint the location where the fire started . Then they start to narrow down the range of possibilities for what the initial case may have been . Carefully examining these locations can sometimes produce physical evidence or residues that lead them to make various conclusions about the fire .
