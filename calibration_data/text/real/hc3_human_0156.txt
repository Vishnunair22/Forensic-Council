For films that use a lot of green / blue screen ( LOTR , Avatar , Avengers ) , the CG background can be made as a 3D object . That way , the only thing that they have to make 3D in post are the actors , and they 're separated already . Also , many older films that used the red and blue glasses were three frames : One that was red , one was blue , and one that was full color . One of the red / blue frames was offset in order to create depth to the image .
