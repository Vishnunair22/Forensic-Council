ACT / SAT / College Board are companies who build tests that roughly judge a student 's proficiency in different subjects . Those tests do n't write themselves , so they hire test makers , and those test makers need to be paid , so they charge prices at a high , but not overly ridiculous price because students have only one alternative , and test preference varies from college to college . So basically ACT / SAT want a high profit . Parchment , on the other hand , is an electronic exchange , and the only people getting paid are web - designers , server maintenance , and tech support , all of which can be outsourced overseas to India or China , thus , the lower price .
