Jehovah's Witnesses are a Christian denomination known for their door-to-door evangelism and their refusal to participate in military service or certain patriotic ceremonies. They believe that the Bible is the inspired word of God and that it provides the basis for their beliefs and practices.\n\nHere are some of the key beliefs of Jehovah's Witnesses:\n\nGod is the supreme being and the creator of the universe. He is known by the name Jehovah.\n\nJesus Christ is the Son of God and the savior of humanity. He was resurrected after his death and now serves as the mediator between God and humanity.\n\nThe Holy Spirit is a force that helps believers understand the truth and live according to God's will.\n\nThe Bible is the ultimate source of truth and authority. It should be read, studied, and followed in order to have a relationship with God.\n\nThe end of the world, or "Armageddon," is coming soon. Only a small group of believers, known as the "anointed," will be resurrected to live forever in a paradise on earth. The rest of humanity will have the opportunity to be resurrected and live forever in a perfect world.\n\nJehovah's Witnesses practice baptism by immersion and observe the Lord's Supper, or Communion, as symbols of their commitment to God.\n\nJehovah's Witnesses do not celebrate Christmas, Easter, or birthdays, as they believe that these holidays have pagan origins and are not supported by the Bible.\n\nJehovah's Witnesses believe that it is important to evangelize and share their beliefs with others in order to bring them to a knowledge of the truth.
