Extreme right wing, also known as far-right or ultra-right, refers to political ideologies or movements that are characterized by their extreme conservatism, authoritarianism, and opposition to progressive or liberal values. These ideologies often hold nationalist, racist, xenophobic, and anti-Semitic beliefs and can promote violence and discrimination against marginalized groups.\n\nIn general, the extreme right wing is characterized by a belief in the superiority of certain groups (often based on race, ethnicity, religion, or nationality) and a desire to maintain the traditional social and political order. Extreme right wing ideologies often reject the principles of democracy and support authoritarian forms of government. They may also advocate for policies that would limit the rights and freedoms of certain groups, such as immigrants, LGBTQ+ people, and minority groups.\n\nIt's important to note that extreme right wing ideologies are often considered to be outside the mainstream of political thought and can be associated with extremist and hate groups.
