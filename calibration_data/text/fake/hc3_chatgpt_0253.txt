Jehovah's Witnesses are a Christian denomination known for their door-to-door evangelizing and their refusal to accept blood transfusions. They believe in one God, whom they refer to as Jehovah, and that Jesus Christ is his son and the savior of humanity. They believe that the Bible is the inspired word of God and is the source of truth for their beliefs.\n\nJehovah's Witnesses follow the teachings of the Bible and believe that only they are fulfilling the requirements of true Christianity. They believe that human governments and institutions are controlled by Satan and that only they have the truth about God's kingdom. They believe that the end of the world is imminent and that only a select group of people, known as Jehovah's Witnesses, will be saved and given eternal life in a paradise on earth.\n\nJehovah's Witnesses reject the concept of the Trinity, the belief that God is three persons in one. They also reject the belief in hell as a place of eternal punishment and believe that the wicked will be destroyed and cease to exist. They do not celebrate holidays such as Christmas and Easter, which they view as pagan in origin, and they do not participate in military service or take oaths, such as pledging allegiance to a flag.
