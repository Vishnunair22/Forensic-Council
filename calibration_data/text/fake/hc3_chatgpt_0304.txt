There are more than 600 muscles in the human body. These muscles can be divided into different categories based on their location and function.\n\nThere are three main types of muscles in the body:\n\nSkeletal muscles: These muscles are attached to the bones and are responsible for movement. They are also known as voluntary muscles because we have control over them and can consciously move them.\n\nCardiac muscles: These muscles are found in the heart and are responsible for pumping blood throughout the body. Cardiac muscles are involuntary, meaning that we do not have conscious control over them.\n\nSmooth muscles: These muscles are found in the walls of organs such as the stomach, intestines, and blood vessels. They are responsible for moving substances through these organs and are also involuntary.\n\nSome of the major muscle groups in the body include the biceps, triceps, quadriceps, and hamstrings in the arms and legs, and the abdominal and back muscles.
