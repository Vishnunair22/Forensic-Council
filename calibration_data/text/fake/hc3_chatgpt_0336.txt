Metal is a genre of rock music that is characterized by its heavy and often aggressive sound, as well as its use of distorted electric guitars and fast-paced tempos. Metal music often features prominent guitar solos, and lyrics that explore themes such as rebellion, social issues, and personal struggles. Some subgenres of metal, such as thrash metal and death metal, are known for their extremely fast tempos and harsh vocals. Other subgenres, such as power metal and doom metal, are known for their epic and grandiose sound, with slower tempos and more atmospheric elements. Overall, metal music is known for its intensity and energy, and is often associated with a rebellious or countercultural attitude.
