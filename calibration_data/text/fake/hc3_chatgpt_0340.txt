An irregular heartbeat, or arrhythmia, is a condition in which the heart beats too quickly, too slowly, or in an irregular pattern. A premature ventricular contraction (PVC) is a type of arrhythmia that occurs when the heart's lower chambers (the ventricles) contract before they should. This can cause the heart to beat too quickly or in an irregular pattern. PVCs can sometimes be felt as a fluttering or "skipped" beat in the chest. In some cases, PVCs may cause symptoms such as dizziness, lightheadedness, or chest discomfort. However, many people with PVCs do not experience any symptoms. PVCs are generally not considered to be a serious condition, but they can be a sign of an underlying heart problem and may need to be treated if they occur frequently or cause symptoms. It is important to discuss any concerns about irregular heartbeats with a healthcare provider.
