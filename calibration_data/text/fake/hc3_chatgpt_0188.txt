The Mohawk people are an indigenous nation with a long and rich history. They are a part of the Iroquois Confederacy, a group of six nations that have traditionally inhabited the northeastern region of North America, including parts of present-day New York, Ontario, Quebec, and Wisconsin. The Mohawk Nation is the largest of the Six Nations, and it is divided into three distinct communities: the Mohawks of Akwesasne, the Mohawks of Kahnawake, and the Mohawks of Tyendinaga. The Mohawks of Akwesasne straddle the border between the United States and Canada, with communities on both sides of the St. Lawrence River. The Mohawks of Kahnawake and the Mohawks of Tyendinaga are both located in Quebec, Canada. In addition to these communities, there are also Mohawk people living in other parts of the United States and Canada, as well as in other countries around the world.
