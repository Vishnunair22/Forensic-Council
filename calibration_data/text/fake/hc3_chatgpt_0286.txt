Base jumping stands for "building, antenna, span, and earth," which refers to the four types of fixed objects that base jumpers jump from. Base jumping is a type of extreme sports in which participants jump from a fixed structure or object, such as a building, antenna, bridge, or cliff, and parachute down to the ground. It is considered an extremely dangerous activity, as there is a high risk of injury or death due to the high speeds and altitudes involved. Base jumpers must have specialized training and equipment to participate in the sport, and it is often illegal due to the risks involved.
