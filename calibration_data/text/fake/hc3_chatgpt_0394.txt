A strategic game is a type of game in which the players' decisions and actions have a significant impact on the outcome of the game. These games often involve a high level of planning and strategy, as players must consider the actions of their opponents and try to anticipate their moves. Strategic games can be played with a physical board or digitally, and they can be based on a variety of themes and genres, including war, economics, politics, and sports. Some examples of strategic games include chess, Go, Risk, and poker.
