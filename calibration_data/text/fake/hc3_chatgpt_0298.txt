Osteonecrosis of the jaw, also known as jawbone death, is a condition in which the jawbone tissue dies due to a lack of blood flow. It can be a side effect of certain medical treatments, such as chemotherapy or radiation therapy, or it can occur due to other underlying conditions, such as diabetes or kidney disease. Osteonecrosis of the jaw can also be caused by dental procedures that involve the extraction of teeth or the placement of dental implants.\n\nSymptoms of osteonecrosis of the jaw may include pain in the jaw, loose teeth, exposed bone, and difficulty eating and speaking. Treatment may involve antibiotics to treat any infection, medications to improve blood flow, and surgery to remove dead bone tissue and repair the jaw. It is important to seek medical attention if you have any symptoms of osteonecrosis of the jaw, as the condition can cause significant functional and aesthetic problems if left untreated.
