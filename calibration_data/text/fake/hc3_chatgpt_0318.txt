Burgundy is a deep, reddish-purple color, similar to a dark red or a red-wine color. It is often associated with luxury and sophistication. The color is named after the Burgundy region of France, which is known for producing red wine. In the RGB color model, burgundy is represented by a combination of red, green, and blue values of 128, 0, and 32, respectively. In the CMYK color model, it is represented by a combination of cyan, magenta, yellow, and black values of 0, 100, 50, and 50, respectively.
