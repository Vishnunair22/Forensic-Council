The sign for degrees is the degree symbol, which looks like a small circle with a horizontal line through it. It is typically used to represent temperature, angles, or other measurements in a variety of fields, including mathematics, science, and engineering. For example, the temperature of water boiling at sea level is 212°F (degrees Fahrenheit), and a right angle is 90° (degrees). The degree symbol is usually typed using the "degree" key on a keyboard, or by using a special character code or symbol shortcut, depending on the software or application being used.
