"What It Takes" is a song by the American rock band Aerosmith. It was released in 1990 as the third single from their critically acclaimed album "Pump". The song was written by Steven Tyler, Tom Hamilton, and Joe Perry, and it was produced by Bruce Fairbairn. It was released as a single on October 23, 1990 and it reached the top 40 in the United States and Canada. The song features a mix of heavy guitar riffs and catchy melodies, and it is considered one of Aerosmith's signature songs.
