Base jumping is an extreme sport in which individuals jump from fixed structures or cliffs using a parachute or wingsuit. The acronym "BASE" stands for the four categories of fixed objects from which base jumpers typically jump: building, antenna, span (bridge), and earth (cliff).\n\nBase jumping is considered to be a high-risk activity due to the potential for equipment failure or human error, and fatalities are not uncommon. As a result, base jumping is often illegal or restricted in many areas, and those who participate in the sport do so at their own risk.
