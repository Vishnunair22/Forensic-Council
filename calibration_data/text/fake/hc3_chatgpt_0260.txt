Honey bee propolis is a sticky, resinous substance that honey bees collect from trees and plants. They use it to seal cracks and gaps in their hive, as well as to inhibit the growth of bacteria, fungi, and other microorganisms. Propolis is made up of a mixture of plant resins, waxes, and other organic compounds, and it has a range of potential health benefits when used as a dietary supplement or topical treatment. Some people use propolis to boost the immune system, reduce inflammation, or treat wounds, while others use it for its alleged antiviral, antimicrobial, and antioxidant properties. However, more research is needed to fully understand the effectiveness and safety of propolis. It is important to note that propolis may cause allergic reactions in some individuals, and it should be used with caution.
