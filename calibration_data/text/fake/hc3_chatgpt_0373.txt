In Catholic theology, the gifts of the Holy Spirit are special graces given to believers by the Holy Spirit to help them grow in their faith and serve the Church. These gifts are often described in the New Testament, particularly in the letter of Saint Paul to the Galatians (Galatians 5:22-23).\n\nThe traditional list of the seven gifts of the Holy Spirit, as expressed in the Catechism of the Catholic Church, is as follows:\n\nWisdom: helps us to see the world and all things in it as God does, with a deeper understanding and insight.\nUnderstanding: enables us to grasp the deeper meaning and significance of what we believe.\nCounsel: helps us to discern God's will for our lives and to make good decisions.\nFortitude: gives us the courage and strength to persevere in doing what is right, even in the face of adversity or temptation.\nKnowledge: helps us to grow in our understanding of God and his ways.\nPiety: helps us to have a deep respect and devotion to God, and to express this through prayer and other forms of religious practice.\nFear of the Lord: helps us to have a healthy sense of awe and reverence for God, and to live in a way that is pleasing to him.\nThese gifts are not something that we can earn or achieve on our own, but rather they are given to us by the Holy Spirit as a way of helping us to grow in our relationship with God and to serve others.
