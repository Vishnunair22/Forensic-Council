There are more than 600 muscles in the human body. These muscles can be classified into three main types: skeletal muscles, smooth muscles, and cardiac muscles.\n\nSkeletal muscles are responsible for voluntary movement and are attached to the bones of the skeleton. They are responsible for movements such as walking, running, and lifting. There are approximately 640 skeletal muscles in the human body.\n\nSmooth muscles are found in the walls of organs and vessels, and they help to control the movement of substances through the body. Smooth muscles are responsible for involuntary movements, such as the contraction of the muscles in the walls of the digestive tract to move food through the body. There are about 100 smooth muscles in the human body.\n\nCardiac muscle is found only in the heart and is responsible for pumping blood throughout the body. Cardiac muscle is a type of involuntary muscle and is responsible for the rhythmic contractions of the heart. There is only one cardiac muscle in the body, located in the heart.
