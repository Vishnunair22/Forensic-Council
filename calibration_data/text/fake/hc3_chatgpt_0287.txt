Base jumping is an extreme sport in which participants jump from fixed objects, such as buildings, bridges, and cliffs, using a parachute to break their fall. The name "base jumping" is derived from the four categories of fixed objects from which the sport is typically performed: buildings, antennae, spans (bridges), and earth (cliffs). The acronym "BASE" stands for these four categories. Base jumping is considered one of the most dangerous extreme sports, due to the high speeds and altitudes reached, as well as the inherent risks associated with parachute jumping. It is illegal in many areas and requires specialized training and equipment to be done safely.
