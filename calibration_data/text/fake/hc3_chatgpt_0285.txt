There are 13 stripes on the American flag. The flag has seven red stripes and six white stripes, which represent the 13 original colonies that declared independence from Great Britain and became the United States. The red stripes represent valor and bravery, while the white stripes represent purity and innocence. The blue field in the upper left corner of the flag contains 50 white, five-pointed stars, which represent the 50 states in the Union. The stars are arranged in nine rows of alternating five and six stars, and are set against a blue field representing vigilance, perseverance, and justice.
