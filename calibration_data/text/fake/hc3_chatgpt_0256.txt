Turkey is a country located in the Eastern Mediterranean region, bordered by eight countries: Bulgaria to the northwest, Greece to the west, Georgia to the northeast, Armenia, Azerbaijan, and Iran to the east, Iraq and Syria to the southeast. It is situated on the Anatolian Peninsula and is a transcontinental country, with a small part in Europe and the majority of its territory in Asia. The capital of Turkey is Ankara, and the largest city is Istanbul. Turkey is a member of the United Nations, NATO, and the Council of Europe.
