Burgundy is a dark red color. It is often described as a deep, reddish-purple or wine color. The exact hue of burgundy can vary somewhat, but it is generally considered to be a dark, rich color that is slightly warmer and less blue than traditional reds. Burgundy is named after a wine-producing region in France and is often used to describe red wines with a similar color. It is also a popular color for clothing, accessories, and home decor.
