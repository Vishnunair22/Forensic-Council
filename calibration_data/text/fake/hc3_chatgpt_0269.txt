Sodium hypochlorite is a chemical compound that is often used as a disinfectant or bleach. It is a clear, slightly yellowish solution with a strong, chlorine-like odor. It is commonly known as "chlorine bleach" and is used to kill germs, bacteria, and other microorganisms on surfaces and in water. It is often used in households to clean and sanitize laundry, dishes, and surfaces. It is also used in swimming pools to kill bacteria and algae. Sodium hypochlorite is a strong oxidizing agent and can be corrosive to certain materials. It should be handled with caution and stored in a cool, dry place.
