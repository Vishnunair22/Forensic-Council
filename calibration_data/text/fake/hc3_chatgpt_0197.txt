In a dim sum restaurant, small plates of food, called dim sum, are traditionally served on small carts that are pushed around the restaurant for customers to choose from. The carts typically feature a variety of steamed, fried, and baked dim sum dishes, such as dumplings, buns, and savory pastries.\n\nTo order dim sum at a restaurant, customers are usually seated at a table and given a menu with a list of dim sum dishes. They can then choose which dishes they would like to order, and the server will bring them to the table. Some dim sum restaurants also offer a buffet-style setup, where customers can go to the buffet to select the dim sum dishes they want.\n\nIn addition to dim sum, many dim sum restaurants also offer other dishes, such as soups, noodles, and rice dishes. Customers may order these dishes in the same way as they would order dim sum.\n\nIt is common for dim sum restaurants to operate using the "yum cha" style of service, which involves frequent refills of tea and small plates of food being brought to the table throughout the meal. This allows customers to try a variety of dishes and enjoy a leisurely dining experience.
