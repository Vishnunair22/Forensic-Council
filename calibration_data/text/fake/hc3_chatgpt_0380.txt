A store confectioner is a person who works in a store that sells confections, which are sweet foods such as candy, chocolate, and pastries. Store confectioners may be responsible for preparing and selling these sweet treats, as well as keeping the store clean and organized. They may also be responsible for customer service, handling money, and keeping track of inventory. Store confectioners may work in a variety of settings, including candy stores, bakeries, and ice cream shops. They may also work in supermarkets or other retail stores that sell a variety of food products, including confections.
