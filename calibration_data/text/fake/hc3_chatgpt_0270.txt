Sodium hypochlorite is a chemical compound that is commonly used as a disinfectant and bleaching agent. It is a clear, greenish-yellow liquid that has a strong, chlorine-like odor. Sodium hypochlorite is often sold as a solution in water, with the concentration of the active ingredient varying depending on the intended use.\n\nIn its most common form, sodium hypochlorite solution is used as a household cleaner and disinfectant. It is effective at killing a wide range of bacteria, viruses, and other microorganisms on surfaces and in water. It is also often used to bleach clothing and other materials.\n\nSodium hypochlorite can be corrosive and can cause skin and eye irritation, so it is important to handle it with care and to follow the instructions on the label when using it. It is also important to store it in a cool, dry place, away from heat and flame, as it can release chlorine gas if it comes into contact with certain materials.
