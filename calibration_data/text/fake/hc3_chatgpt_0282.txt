Primary care medicine, also known as primary care, is a branch of medicine that focuses on the general health needs of individuals and families. It is typically the first point of contact for people seeking medical attention, and it is designed to be accessible, continuous, and comprehensive. Primary care physicians, also known as primary care providers, are trained to diagnose and treat a wide range of medical conditions and to provide preventive care, such as immunizations and screenings. They may also coordinate care with specialists as needed. Primary care medicine is an important part of the healthcare system as it helps to ensure that people receive timely and appropriate care for their medical needs.
