Black Friday is a shopping holiday that traditionally takes place on the day after Thanksgiving in the United States. It is not directly related to Christmas, but it is a popular time for holiday shopping and many retailers offer discounts and promotions on a wide range of products. The exact date of Black Friday varies from year to year, but it typically falls on the fourth Friday in November. This year, Black Friday is on November 26th. Some retailers may begin offering Black Friday deals and promotions earlier in the week or even earlier in the month, so it is a good idea to keep an eye out for advertisements and check with your favorite stores to see when their Black Friday sales will begin.
