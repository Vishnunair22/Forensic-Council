There are 13 stripes on the American flag. The stripes represent the original 13 colonies that declared independence from Great Britain and formed the United States. The flag has 50 stars, which represent the 50 states of the United States. The stars are arranged in nine rows of five stars each on a blue field in the upper left corner of the flag. The red and white stripes are arranged horizontally, with seven red stripes and six white stripes. The colors red, white, and blue were chosen for the American flag because they were the colors of the Great Seal of the United States, which was adopted in 1782.
