A notary is a public official who is authorized to witness the signing of documents and to administer oaths. In many countries, a notary is also responsible for authenticating and certifying the validity of documents, such as wills, deeds, and contracts. A notary's role is to ensure that the parties involved in a transaction are who they say they are and that they understand the contents of the document they are signing. The notary's signature and seal on a document serve as evidence that the document has been properly executed and can be relied upon in a court of law. In some jurisdictions, a notary may also be authorized to perform other legal tasks, such as conducting marriage ceremonies or serving as an executor of an estate.
