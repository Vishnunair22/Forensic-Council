Human intelligence is the mental ability to think, learn, and understand concepts. It is what enables us to process, analyze, and use information, as well as to reason, plan, solve problems, and make decisions. Intelligence is a complex and multifaceted concept that is difficult to define and measure, but it is generally considered to include a range of cognitive abilities such as verbal and mathematical aptitude, spatial awareness, memory, and problem-solving skills. Intelligence is an important aspect of human behavior that is thought to be influenced by a combination of genetic and environmental factors.
