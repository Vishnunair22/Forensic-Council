Loxahatchee is a small town located in Palm Beach County, Florida, United States. It is situated about 45 miles north of Miami and about 20 miles west of West Palm Beach. It is known for its natural beauty, with many parks and preserves areas, and is home to the Loxahatchee National Wildlife Refuge, a protected area for endangered and threatened species. Loxahatchee is also located near many popular attractions, such as Lion Country Safari, the Palm Beach Zoo, and the Morikami Museum and Japanese Gardens.
