Vitamin A is a nutrient that is essential for maintaining healthy vision, immune system function, and healthy skin. It is also important for the proper functioning of the heart, lungs, kidneys, and other organs. Vitamin A is found in a variety of foods, including liver, fish, eggs, milk, and fortified cereals. It is also available as a dietary supplement.\n\nThere are two main forms of vitamin A:\n\nRetinol: This is the animal form of vitamin A and is found in animal-derived foods such as liver, egg yolks, and whole milk.\n\nCarotenoids: These are plant pigments that can be converted into vitamin A by the body. Beta-carotene is the most well-known carotenoid and is found in yellow, orange, and dark green vegetables such as carrots, sweet potatoes, and spinach.\n\nVitamin A is important for maintaining good vision, especially in low light conditions. It is also essential for the proper functioning of the immune system and for the growth and development of cells and tissues. Additionally, vitamin A plays a role in the production of collagen, which is important for healthy skin and mucous membranes.\n\nA deficiency in vitamin A can cause vision problems, dry skin, and an increased risk of infections. It is important to get enough vitamin A in your diet to maintain good health. However, it is also possible to get too much vitamin A, which can cause side effects such as nausea, vomiting, and liver damage. It is important to talk to a healthcare professional before starting a vitamin A supplement to determine the appropriate dosage for you.
