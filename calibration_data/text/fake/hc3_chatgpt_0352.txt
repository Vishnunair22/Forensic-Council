In poetry, a stanza is a group of lines that form a unit within a poem. It is similar to a paragraph in prose, but is usually made up of a set number of lines and has a specific rhyme and meter pattern. Stanzas are often used to divide a poem into sections, to create a specific rhythm or rhyme scheme, or to convey a change in the poem's content or tone. Some common types of stanzas include rhymed couplets, tercets, quatrains, and cinquains. Each type of stanza has its own unique rhyme and meter pattern, and poets may use different stanza forms to achieve a variety of effects in their poetry.
