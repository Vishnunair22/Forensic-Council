In the television show "Grey's Anatomy," George O'Malley was a character who was initially introduced as an intern at the fictional Grey Sloan Memorial Hospital. He was portrayed by actor T.R. Knight.\n\nIn the fifth season of the show, George is involved in a serious accident and is severely injured. He eventually dies from his injuries, after being hit by a bus while trying to save a stranger. The character's death was a major plot point in the show and was met with widespread acclaim from both critics and fans.
