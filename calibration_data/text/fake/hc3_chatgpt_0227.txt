Metformin is a medication that is used to treat type 2 diabetes. It is a type of drug called a biguanide, which works by decreasing the amount of sugar produced by the liver and increasing the sensitivity of muscle cells to insulin, a hormone that helps regulate blood sugar levels in the body. Metformin is typically used in combination with lifestyle changes, such as diet and exercise, to help lower blood sugar levels in people with type 2 diabetes. It may also be used to treat other conditions, such as polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS) and non-alcoholic fatty liver disease (NAFLD).
