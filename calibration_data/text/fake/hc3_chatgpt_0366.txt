A medallion guarantee is a stamp or signature on a document that serves as a guarantee of the authenticity and genuineness of the document. It is typically found on financial documents such as checks, money orders, and drafts, and is often required by banks and other financial institutions to ensure the security of financial transactions. The medallion guarantee is typically provided by a financial institution or other authorized organization, and is intended to protect against fraud and counterfeiting. In order to qualify for a medallion guarantee, the document in question must meet certain standards of authenticity and security, and the guarantee may be subject to certain fees or other charges.
