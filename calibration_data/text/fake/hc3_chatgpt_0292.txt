Dmitri Mendeleev was a Russian chemist and inventor who is best known for creating the periodic table of elements. The periodic table is a chart that arranges the elements in order of increasing atomic number and groups them according to their chemical properties. Mendeleev's periodic table, which he published in 1869, helped to organize and understand the relationships between the different elements, and it remains an important tool for chemists today. Mendeleev also made significant contributions to the fields of metallurgy, geology, and economics, and he is considered one of the founders of modern chemistry.
