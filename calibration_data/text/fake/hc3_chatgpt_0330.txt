World War II was caused by a number of factors, including the aggressive actions of Nazi Germany, led by dictator Adolf Hitler, and the Axis powers (Italy and Japan). The rise of fascist regimes in Europe and Asia, along with the failure of appeasement policies, contributed to the outbreak of the war.\n\nIn the 1930s, Hitler and the Nazi Party came to power in Germany and began implementing their ideology, which included the belief in the superiority of the Aryan race and the desire to create a "new order" in Europe through military conquest. Hitler also sought to annex territory inhabited by ethnic Germans, which led to the invasion and annexation of Austria in 1938 and the annexation of the Sudetenland in Czechoslovakia in 1938.\n\nIn September 1939, Germany invaded Poland, which led Britain and France to declare war on Germany. Italy and Japan later joined the Axis powers, and the war quickly spread to other parts of Europe, Africa, and Asia. The war ended in 1945 with the defeat of the Axis powers.
