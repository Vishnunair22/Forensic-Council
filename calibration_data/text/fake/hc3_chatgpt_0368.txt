A medallion guarantee is a form of endorsement that is placed on a check or money order to guarantee that it will be honored by the bank or financial institution upon which it is drawn. It is typically a small stamp or seal that is applied to the check or money order by a bank or other financial institution, and it serves as a guarantee that the check or money order is genuine and will be paid when presented for payment. The medallion guarantee is intended to provide additional security and protection against fraudulent or altered checks, and it is often required by banks and other financial institutions when accepting large checks or money orders for deposit or cashing.
