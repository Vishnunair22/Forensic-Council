Honey bee propolis is a resinous substance that honey bees collect from tree buds, sap flows, and other botanical sources. It is used by the bees to seal small gaps in the hive and to protect against diseases and parasites. Propolis has a number of beneficial properties, including antimicrobial, anti-inflammatory, and antiviral effects. It is sometimes used in natural medicine as a dietary supplement or as a topical treatment for various ailments. It is also used in some commercial products, such as mouthwashes and toothpaste, due to its antimicrobial properties. However, it is important to note that propolis can cause allergic reactions in some people, so it is important to be cautious when using it.
