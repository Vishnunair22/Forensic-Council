Honey bee propolis is a sticky, resinous substance that honey bees collect from plants and trees. It is used by bees to seal gaps in the hive, protect against disease, and deter predators. Propolis is composed of a mixture of plant resins, waxes, and other compounds that vary depending on the plants and trees that the bees visit. It is known for its antimicrobial and antioxidant properties, and has been used in traditional medicine for centuries. Some people use propolis as a natural remedy for a variety of ailments, such as wounds, infections, and inflammation. However, there is limited scientific evidence to support these uses, and more research is needed to fully understand the potential health benefits of propolis.
