Metal music is a genre of rock music that typically features distorted and amplified guitar riffs, fast-paced tempos, and aggressive or intense lyrics. It often incorporates elements of other musical styles, such as classical music and folk music, and sometimes includes elements of horror, science fiction, and fantasy. Metal music can be further divided into subgenres, such as heavy metal, thrash metal, and death metal, each of which has its own distinctive characteristics and themes.\n\nIn general, metal music is known for its high-energy and intense sound, and it often addresses themes of rebellion, individualism, and social or political issues. Some metal lyrics may also explore more personal themes, such as relationships, self-discovery, and personal struggles. Metal music often appeals to fans who enjoy its raw, powerful sound and its emphasis on individual expression and emotion.
