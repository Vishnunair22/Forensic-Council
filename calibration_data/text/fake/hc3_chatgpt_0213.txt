Milk is a nutritious liquid produced by mammals such as cows, goats, and humans to feed their young. It is a rich source of proteins, vitamins, and minerals.\n\nThe main components of milk are water, lactose (a sugar), fat, and protein. Milk also contains small amounts of vitamins and minerals, such as vitamin D, calcium, and phosphorous. The exact composition of milk can vary depending on the species of mammal, its diet, and the stage of lactation.\n\nCow's milk is the most commonly consumed type of milk, and it is composed of approximately 87% water, 4.7% lactose, 3.4% fat, and 3.3% protein. Goat's milk and human milk have slightly different compositions, but they also contain similar nutrients.\n\nMilk is an important source of nutrients for humans and is often consumed on its own or used in a variety of food products, such as cheese, yogurt, and ice cream.
