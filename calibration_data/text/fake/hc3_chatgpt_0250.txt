Cono Sur (meaning "Southern Cone" in Spanish) is a term that refers to the southern cone of South America, which includes the following countries:\n\nArgentina\nChile\nUruguay\nParaguay\nSouthern Brazil (specifically, the states of Paraná, Santa Catarina, and Rio Grande do Sul)\nThese countries are known for their shared cultural, economic, and political characteristics, as well as their shared history and geography. The term "Cono Sur" is often used to refer to the region as a whole or to the grouping of these countries in regional organizations such as the Mercosur trade bloc.
