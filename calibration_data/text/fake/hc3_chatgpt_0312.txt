It is difficult to give a precise estimate of the number of Muslims in the United Kingdom, as national statistics on religion are not collected in the UK. However, estimates from sources such as the Office for National Statistics and the Pew Research Center suggest that Muslims make up around 5% of the total population of the UK. This would translate to approximately 3.45 million Muslims living in the country. It is worth noting that the Muslim population in the UK is diverse and includes people from a variety of ethnic and national backgrounds.
