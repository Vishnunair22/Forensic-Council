The Beatles released "I Want to Hold Your Hand" in 1964. It was the band's first single released in the United States and became a major hit, reaching the top of the charts and helping to launch the "British Invasion" of the US music scene. The song was written by John Lennon and Paul McCartney and was recorded at EMI's Abbey Road Studios in London. It was released in the UK in November 1963 and in the US in December of that year.
