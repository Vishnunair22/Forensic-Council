Turkey is a country located in the Middle East and Southeast Europe. It is situated at the crossroads of Europe and Asia, with a small portion of its territory (3%) in Europe and the majority (97%) in Asia. Turkey is bordered by eight countries: Bulgaria to the northwest, Greece to the west, Georgia to the northeast, Armenia, Azerbaijan, and Iran to the east, Iraq and Syria to the southeast. The country has a long and varied history, with roots that go back to ancient civilizations such as the Hittites, Persians, and Romans. Today, Turkey is a modern, democratic, and secular nation with a diverse population and a rich cultural heritage.
