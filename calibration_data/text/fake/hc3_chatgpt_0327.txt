A notary is a public officer who is authorized to perform certain legal formalities, such as witnessing the signing of documents and administering oaths. Notaries are often required to authenticate documents for use in other countries, and they are also often called upon to serve as impartial witnesses to the signing of important documents, such as wills, deeds, contracts, and mortgages. In some jurisdictions, notaries may also be authorized to perform other duties, such as taking affidavits, certifying copies of documents, and performing marriage ceremonies.
