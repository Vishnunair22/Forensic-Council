When you exercise, your body uses up energy and can become tired. When you push yourself to do a very intense workout, your body can become overwhelmed and not be able to keep up with the physical demands you are putting on it. This can cause a number of physical symptoms, including feeling sick to your stomach and throwing up. 
Throwing up after a workout can also be a sign of overexertion, which means that you have pushed yourself too hard and your body is not able to handle the stress. Overexertion can cause your body to release certain hormones and chemicals that can make you feel sick and cause you to throw up. 
It's important to listen to your body and not push yourself too hard when you are exercising. It's okay to challenge yourself and work hard, but it's also important to take breaks and rest when you need to, and to hydrate and fuel your body properly before and after your workouts.
