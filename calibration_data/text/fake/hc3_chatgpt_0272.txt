Rough and Ready, California is a small unincorporated community located in Nevada County, California, United States. It is situated in the Sierra Nevada mountain range, about 25 miles (40 km) northeast of the city of Grass Valley. The community is named after General Zachary Taylor, whose nickname was "Old Rough and Ready." It was founded in the mid-1800s during the California Gold Rush, and at one time was home to a thriving mining industry. Today, Rough and Ready is a small community with a population of about 1,000 people. It is located off of State Route 20, which runs through the heart of the community.
