An irregular heartbeat, also known as an arrhythmia, is a condition in which the heart beats too fast, too slow, or in an irregular pattern. A premature ventricular contraction (PVC) is a type of arrhythmia in which the heart's lower chambers (ventricles) contract before they should. This can cause a noticeable pause or "skipped beat" in the heartbeat. PVCs are often benign and do not cause any symptoms, but they can sometimes lead to more serious arrhythmias or heart problems. If you are experiencing symptoms such as a rapid or irregular heartbeat, chest pain, or shortness of breath, you should speak to a healthcare provider as soon as possible.
