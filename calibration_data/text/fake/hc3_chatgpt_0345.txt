Lyme disease is caused by the bacterium Borrelia burgdorferi and is transmitted to humans through the bite of infected ticks. The most common symptoms of Lyme disease are fever, headache, fatigue, and a characteristic skin rash called erythema migrans. If left untreated, the infection can spread to the joints, heart, and nervous system, leading to more severe symptoms such as joint pain, irregular heartbeat, and facial paralysis.\n\nSome possible side effects of treatment for Lyme disease may include:\n\nNausea or upset stomach\nRash or hives\nAllergic reactions, such as difficulty breathing, swelling of the face, lips, tongue, or throat\nJoint pain or swelling\nFever or chills\nDizziness\nIt is important to consult a healthcare provider for proper diagnosis and treatment of Lyme disease. It is also important to follow the prescribed treatment plan to ensure that the infection is adequately treated and to minimize the risk of serious complications.
