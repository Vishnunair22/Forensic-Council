Area code 217 is an area code in the North American Numbering Plan (NANP) for the central part of the state of Illinois, including the cities of Champaign, Decatur, and Springfield. It was one of the original area codes established in 1947, and it covers an area that includes the following counties: Cass, Christian, Clark, Coles, Cumberland, De Witt, Douglas, Edgar, Effingham, Fayette, Ford, Greene, Jasper, Logan, Macon, Macoupin, Mason, Menard, Moultrie, Piatt, Sangamon, Schuyler, Shelby, and Vermilion.
