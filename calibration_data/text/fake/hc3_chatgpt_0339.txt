The Beatles released the song "I Want to Hold Your Hand" in 1964. It was their first single to be released in the United States, and it became a major hit, reaching the top of the charts and helping to introduce the band to a wider audience. The song was written by John Lennon and Paul McCartney and was recorded at EMI Studios in London. It was released on the album "Meet the Beatles!" and has since become one of the most iconic and beloved songs in the history of rock music.
