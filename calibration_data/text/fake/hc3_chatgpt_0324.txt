The capacity of AT&T Stadium, also known as Cowboy Stadium, is 80,000 for football games. However, the capacity can be expanded to as much as 105,000 for special events. AT&T Stadium is a multi-purpose stadium located in Arlington, Texas and is the home field of the Dallas Cowboys professional football team. It has also been used for other events such as concerts, college football games, and other sporting events. The stadium is known for its large size and state-of-the-art amenities, including a retractable roof and a massive high-definition video screen.
