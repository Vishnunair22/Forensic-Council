People of Japanese descent who are citizens or residents of the United States are often referred to as Japanese Americans. This term applies to people who are either immigrants to the United States from Japan or are descendants of Japanese immigrants. Japanese Americans can have a wide range of cultural backgrounds, experiences, and identities, as they may have been born in the United States, in Japan, or in other countries, and may have varying degrees of connection to Japanese culture and heritage. Some Japanese Americans may also identify as being part of other ethnic or racial groups, such as Asian American or multiracial.
