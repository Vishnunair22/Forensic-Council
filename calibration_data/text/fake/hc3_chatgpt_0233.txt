Afghan cuisine is a mix of Middle Eastern and Central Asian flavors and influences, with an emphasis on fresh, natural ingredients. Some common foods in Afghanistan include:\n\nKabuli palaw: a dish made with steamed rice and topped with carrots, raisins, and nuts, often served with lamb or chicken\nQabuli palaw: a similar dish to Kabuli palaw, but made with ground meat and spices\nAush: a type of soup or stew made with noodles, vegetables, and meat\nBolani: a type of flatbread filled with vegetables such as potatoes, onions, and peas\nMantu: a type of dumpling filled with ground meat and onions, served with a yogurt sauce\nQorma: a type of stew made with meat and vegetables, cooked in a sauce made with yogurt or sour cream\nFirni: a dessert made with ground rice, milk, and sugar, often flavored with rose water or cardamom\nAfghan cuisine is also known for its use of spices such as cumin, coriander, and turmeric, as well as herbs like mint and cilantro.
