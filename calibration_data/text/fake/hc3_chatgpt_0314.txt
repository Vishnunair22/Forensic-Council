The symbol for degrees is a small raised circle, or degree symbol, °. It is used to represent degrees of measurement, such as degrees of temperature or angles. For example, if you wanted to write that a temperature is 30 degrees Celsius, you would write it as 30°C. If you wanted to write that an angle measures 60 degrees, you would write it as 60°. The degree symbol is also used in mathematics to denote angles, arc lengths, and other measurements.
