Lyme disease is caused by the bacterium Borrelia burgdorferi, which is transmitted to humans through the bite of infected ticks. The most common symptoms of Lyme disease include fever, chills, headache, fatigue, muscle and joint pain, and a characteristic rash called erythema migrans. If left untreated, the infection can spread to other parts of the body and cause more serious symptoms, such as facial paralysis, heart palpitations, and meningitis.\n\nOther possible side effects of Lyme disease may include:\n\nJoint inflammation (Lyme arthritis)\nNumbness or weakness in the arms and legs\nMemory problems or difficulty concentrating\nInflammation of the brain and spinal cord (meningitis)\nEye inflammation\nChest pain\nShortness of breath\nRash on other areas of the body\nTingling or shooting pains in the hands or feet\nIf you think you may have been exposed to ticks and are experiencing any of these symptoms, it is important to see a healthcare provider as soon as possible for proper diagnosis and treatment.
