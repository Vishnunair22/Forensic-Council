A medallion guarantee is a form of security or collateral used in the taxi and limousine industry. It is a physical medallion, which is a metal plaque or emblem, that is affixed to the exterior of a taxi or limousine vehicle. The medallion acts as a guarantee that the vehicle has been licensed and approved by the local government to operate as a taxi or limousine. The medallion is typically owned by the taxi or limousine company and is leased to individual drivers. The medallion system is used in some cities to regulate the number of taxi and limousine vehicles operating within the city and to ensure that these vehicles meet certain standards of safety and service. In some cases, the medallion may be required to be transferred or sold through a medallion transfer or sale process, which is typically governed by local laws and regulations.
