The Fundamental Theorem of Calculus is a mathematical theorem that establishes a relationship between the two main branches of calculus: differential calculus and integral calculus. It is used to evaluate definite integrals, which are used to represent the area under a curve or the volume of a solid of revolution. It is also used to find the average value of a function over a given interval, to solve optimization problems, and to find the work done by a force along a curve. Additionally, the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus is used in the solution of differential equations, which are used to model many real-world phenomena, such as the motion of a projectile or the spread of a disease.
