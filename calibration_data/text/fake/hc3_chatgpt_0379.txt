A store confectioner is a person who works in a retail store that sells confections, which are sweet foods such as candy, chocolate, and other sweets. The store confectioner is responsible for preparing, displaying, and selling these items to customers. They may also be responsible for maintaining the cleanliness and appearance of the store, as well as keeping track of inventory and ordering supplies as needed. Some store confectioners may also be responsible for creating and decorating custom confections for special events or occasions.
