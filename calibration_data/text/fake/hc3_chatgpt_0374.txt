The Catholic Church teaches that the gifts of the Holy Spirit are special abilities or endowments that are given to believers in Jesus Christ to help them to grow in holiness and to better serve the body of Christ, the Church. These gifts are often associated with the fruit of the Holy Spirit, which are the qualities that the Holy Spirit produces in the life of a believer.\n\nThe traditional list of the seven gifts of the Holy Spirit is:\n\nWisdom: a gift that helps us to understand the mysteries of faith and to discern God's will for our lives.\nUnderstanding: a gift that helps us to grasp the deeper meaning of the things of God and to see the interconnectedness of all things.\nCounsel: a gift that helps us to give sound and practical advice to others and to make good decisions.\nFortitude: a gift that helps us to persevere in difficult times and to remain faithful to God even in the face of persecution.\nKnowledge: a gift that helps us to deepen our understanding of God and His ways.\nPiety: a gift that helps us to develop a deep and personal relationship with God through prayer and devotion.\nFear of the Lord: a gift that helps us to reverence and respect God and to live in awe of His majesty and power.\nIt is important to note that these gifts are not necessarily tangible abilities or talents, but rather spiritual endowments that help us to grow in our relationship with God and to serve others more effectively.
