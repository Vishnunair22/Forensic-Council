Primary medicine, also known as primary care, is the first point of contact for patients with the healthcare system. It is the foundation of the healthcare system, providing preventive and acute care for individuals and families. Primary care providers, such as primary care physicians, nurse practitioners, and physician assistants, are responsible for managing the overall healthcare of their patients. They diagnose and treat a wide range of medical conditions and injuries, provide preventive care and screenings, and refer patients to specialists when necessary. Primary care is often provided in a clinic or office setting, and is typically less specialized than other types of medical care. It is important for patients to establish a relationship with a primary care provider in order to receive ongoing, comprehensive healthcare.
