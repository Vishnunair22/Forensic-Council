The Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA) is a federal law that provides certain employees with up to 12 weeks of unpaid, job-protected leave per year for certain family and medical reasons, including:\n\nThe birth, adoption, or foster care placement of a child\nThe care of a spouse, child, or parent who has a serious health condition\nA serious health condition that makes the employee unable to perform their job\nTo be eligible for FMLA leave, an employee must have worked for their employer for at least 12 months, have worked at least 1,250 hours over the past 12 months, and work at a location where the company employs at least 50 employees within 75 miles.\n\nThe FMLA requires that the employer continue to provide health insurance for the employee during the leave period, and that the employee be allowed to return to the same or an equivalent job upon their return from leave. The FMLA applies to all public agencies, including local, state, and federal employers, as well as private sector employers who employ 50 or more employees.
