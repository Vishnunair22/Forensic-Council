An irregular heartbeat, also known as an arrhythmia, is a condition in which the heart beats with an abnormal rhythm. A premature ventricular contraction (PVC) is a type of arrhythmia in which the heart's lower chambers (the ventricles) contract earlier than they should. This can cause the heart to beat too fast or too slow, and can cause a range of symptoms, including a racing or fluttering sensation in the chest, lightheadedness, dizziness, and fainting. PVCs can be benign and harmless in some cases, but they can also be a sign of a more serious underlying heart condition. It's important to speak with a healthcare provider if you are experiencing symptoms of an irregular heartbeat or PVC.
