Rough and Ready, California is a small unincorporated community located in Nevada County, California. It is located about 10 miles northwest of Grass Valley, California, and about 50 miles northwest of Sacramento, California. Rough and Ready is known for its history as an independent republic in the mid-19th century, when it briefly seceded from the United States. The community has a population of around 2,000 people and is located in the Sierra Nevada foothills.
