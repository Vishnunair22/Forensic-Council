Tri-tip is a cut of beef from the bottom sirloin of a cow. It is a triangular shaped muscle that is lean and flavorful, with a moderate amount of marbling. It is often roasted or grilled, and is popular in Western cuisine, particularly in California. The tri-tip is typically cut into steaks or roasts and can be prepared using a variety of cooking methods, including grilling, broiling, and roasting. It is often seasoned with a rub or marinade before cooking, and is typically served thinly sliced against the grain.
