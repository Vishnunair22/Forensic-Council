Doctor of Medicine (MD) is a professional degree in medicine that is awarded to individuals who have completed a program of study at a medical school. The MD degree is a postgraduate degree that is recognized by many countries around the world as a qualification for practicing medicine. MD programs typically include coursework in a range of medical subjects, as well as clinical rotations in which students gain hands-on experience in diagnosing and treating patients. After completing an MD program, doctors are required to pass a licensing exam in order to practice medicine in their jurisdiction.
