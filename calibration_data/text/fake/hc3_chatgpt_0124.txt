The United States is a federal democratic republic consisting of 50 states, a capital district, and several territories. It is one of the world's largest and most diverse countries, with a range of geographic features and climates, and a diverse population made up of people from many different cultural, ethnic, and racial backgrounds. The United States has a long history and has played a significant role in world affairs. It is a constitutional republic with a system of government that separates and balances power among its three branches: the legislative, executive, and judicial. The United States is also a capitalist economy, in which individuals and businesses own and control the means of production and trade goods and services in a competitive market.
