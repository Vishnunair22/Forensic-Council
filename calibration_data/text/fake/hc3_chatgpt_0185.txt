RFID tags are powered either by a battery or by the radio frequency (RF) energy that is transmitted by the RFID reader. When an RFID tag is powered by a battery, it can operate independently of the RFID reader and transmit its information to the reader whenever it is in range. When an RFID tag is powered by RF energy, it does not require a battery and is able to transmit its information to the RFID reader when it is within range of the reader's RF field.\n\nRFID tags that are powered by RF energy are known as passive RFID tags. These tags have no internal power source and rely on the RF energy emitted by the RFID reader to power their circuits and transmit their information. Passive RFID tags are generally smaller, cheaper, and have a longer lifespan than active RFID tags, which are powered by a battery.\n\nRFID tags that are powered by a battery are known as active RFID tags. These tags have an internal power source and can transmit their information to the RFID reader over a longer range than passive RFID tags. Active RFID tags are typically more expensive and have a shorter lifespan than passive RFID tags.
