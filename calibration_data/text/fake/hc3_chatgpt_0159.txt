Testosterone is a hormone produced by the male testes and the female ovaries. In men, testosterone is produced in the Leydig cells of the testes, which are located in the testicular tissue. In women, testosterone is produced in the ovaries, which are located in the pelvic region. The production of testosterone is regulated by the hypothalamus and the pituitary gland, which are located in the brain. These glands secrete hormones that stimulate the production of testosterone in the testes and ovaries.
