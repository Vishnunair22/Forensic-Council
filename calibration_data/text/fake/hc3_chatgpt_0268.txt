It is different from the avoirdupois ounce, which is a unit of weight used for most other things. One troy ounce is equivalent to about 1.09714 avoirdupois ounces, or about 10% more than a standard ounce.\n\nIn the precious metals industry, gold is often bought and sold in troy ounces.
