Rough and Ready is a small unincorporated community in Nevada County, California, United States. It is located about 10 miles (16 km) northwest of Grass Valley, at an elevation of 2,200 feet (670 m). The community was founded in the 1850s as a gold mining town, and it was named after General Zachary Taylor, who was nicknamed "Old Rough and Ready" due to his simple and unpretentious style. Today, Rough and Ready is a small rural community with a population of about 2,000 people. It is known for its historic buildings and its annual Independence Day parade, which is one of the oldest in the state.
