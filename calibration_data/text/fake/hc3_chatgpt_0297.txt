In Major League Baseball (MLB), a player hits for the cycle when they get a single, double, triple, and home run in the same game. It is a rare feat that has been accomplished by a small number of players in MLB history. According to data from Baseball-Reference.com, a player has hit for the cycle a total of 327 times in MLB regular season games. In addition, there have been a handful of instances of players hitting for the cycle in the postseason. It is difficult to say exactly how many times this has happened, as records from earlier postseason games are not as complete as those from the regular season.
